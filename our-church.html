Who We Are

Building Christ-Centred Lives, Families and Communities for a Life of Purpose

Life Ministries is a Christian church in Heidelberg, Gauteng. We are part of one Life Ministries family that includes our church and our Christian school, united under a single vision — to help people know Christ, build character, discover purpose, and impact the world around them.

Our Vision

Five Things We're Building Toward

Know Christ

Helping people develop a genuine relationship with Jesus through Biblical teaching, discipleship, prayer and worship.

Build Character

Developing integrity, discipline, responsibility and compassion — equipping people to live out their faith daily.

Discover Purpose

Helping every person discover their God-given gifts and purpose, and encouraging leadership and growth.

Strengthen Families

Supporting families spiritually and practically, and building a healthy church and school community.

Impact the Community

Serving people beyond the church through outreach, practical acts of service, and being a positive local influence.

What to Expect When You Visit

You're welcome exactly as you are. Come as you are dressed, arrive a few minutes early to find a seat, and expect a warm welcome from our church family. Our Sunday services include worship, teaching from God's Word, and a children's ministry programme for the kids.
