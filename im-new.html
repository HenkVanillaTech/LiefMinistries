New Here?

You're Welcome Exactly As You Are

Whether you're new to church altogether or just new to Life Ministries, we're glad you're here. This page will help you know what to expect and how to take your first step in.

First Visit

What to Expect

Come As You Are

There's no dress code here — wear whatever makes you feel comfortable. You'll see everything from casual to smart, and either is perfectly fine.

Arrive a Few Minutes Early

Getting there a little before the service starts gives you time to find a seat and settle in without feeling rushed.

A Warm Welcome

Expect a friendly welcome from our church family — no pressure, just genuine hospitality.

Worship, Word & Kids Ministry

Our Sunday services include worship, teaching from God's Word, and a dedicated children's ministry programme running alongside for the kids.
