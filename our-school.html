Why Choose Life Ministries

A Christian School in Heidelberg, Gauteng — Educating the Whole Child

Life Ministries Christian School is a Christian school in Heidelberg, Gauteng, and we believe that education is about more than academic success — it is about developing the whole child. We provide a nurturing, Christ-centred environment where learners are encouraged to grow in faith, knowledge, character and confidence. Our school strives to create a strong foundation for every learner, equipping them with the values, skills and support they need to reach their full potential and make a positive difference in the world.

School Ethos

We, at Life Ministries Christian School, believe it is a God given responsibility of the parents and the local body of Christ (as revealed in God's Word, the Bible) to obtain an education for our children which is grounded on the moral values contained in the Bible. Life Ministries Christian School uses the Impaq curriculum and in addition thereto, Scripture memory passages, and references to God and Jesus Christ — all designed to help students develop moral character, a sense of accountability, and wisdom in their lives.

Vision

To create a safe and disciplined school environment wherein we can raise young men and women of strong Christian character through Biblical Values, Character building and Academic Excellence.
