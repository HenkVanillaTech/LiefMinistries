Our Ministries

There's a place for everyone at Life Ministries. Whether you're looking to grow in prayer, connect with other young people, get your children involved, dig deeper into Scripture, or serve the community around us — here's where to start.

Intercession Prayer Group

Prayer is at the centre of everything we do. Join fellow believers for early-morning intercession — praying for our church, school, community and nation.

When: Fridays at 05:15
Contact: Juan — 064 879 5868 (RSVP required)

Life Youth

A place for teenagers to belong, ask real questions, and grow in their faith together through worship, teaching and fellowship.

When: Sundays at 17:00 (ages 13–18)
Contact: Tiaan or Susan for more info.

Children's Ministry

A safe, fun, age-appropriate space for children to learn about God's Word through stories, songs and activities during our Sunday services.

When: Sundays, during services.

Bible Studies & Small Groups

Smaller groups meeting throughout the week to study Scripture, ask questions, and grow in community with one another — a great way to go deeper than a Sunday service alone.

Contact us to find a group that fits your week.

Outreach & Community Involvement

We believe faith is meant to be lived out beyond our own walls. Our outreach ministry serves the wider Heidelberg community through practical acts of service — because impacting the community is one of the five pillars of everything Life Ministries stands for.
